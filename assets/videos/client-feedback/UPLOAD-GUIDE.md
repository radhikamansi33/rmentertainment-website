# Client Feedback: video upload guide

This section shows **4 videos** (`video-1.mp4` to `video-4.mp4`) on the Client Feedback page (`client-feedback/`). To add more, add a slot for each in `assets/data/media-manifest.js` under `"client-feedback"`.

Photos for this page live in `assets/images/gallery/client-feedback-*.jpg` and are listed under `"photos"` with `"section": "client-feedback"`.

## Video requirements
- Format: MP4 (H.264 video, AAC audio), under 25 MB each
- Name: exactly `video-1.mp4`, `video-2.mp4`, and so on
- Poster image: `posters/video-1.jpg` and so on
- Only genuine R & M client footage, shared with the client's permission.
