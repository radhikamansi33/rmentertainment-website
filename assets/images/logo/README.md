# Logo

Upload your logo here as **one** of these (the site tries them in this order):

1. `logo.png`
2. `logo.jpg`
3. `logo.jpeg`

Recommended: square or wide logo, transparent PNG, at least 400 px tall.
`logo.svg` is a **placeholder monogram**. It shows only until you upload your own logo,
so delete it afterwards if you like.
