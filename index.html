Questions, answered.

Requirements, scheduling, inclusions, and availability are confirmed during consultation.

Can an entry be personalised?

Yes. Every entry is designed around your occasion, personality, and the atmosphere you want.

Do we need dance experience?

No. We offer beginner-friendly rehearsal guidance and shape movement around your comfort.

Can family members participate?

Yes. Family and friends can join an entry or performance, depending on the concept we agree.

Are virtual sessions available?

Yes, guided online choreography sessions are available. Formats and schedules are confirmed during consultation.

What is a technical rehearsal?

A session that brings timing and venue details together before the celebration: entrances, formations, transitions, and relevant cues.

How do we enquire about availability?

Send an enquiry through the form. Availability is confirmed during consultation, and an enquiry does not confirm a booking.

How is a quotation prepared?

After we understand your celebration, requirements, scheduling, and inclusions are discussed and confirmed. We don't publish fixed package prices.
