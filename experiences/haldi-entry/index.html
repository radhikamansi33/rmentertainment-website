Experiences / Haldi & Mehndi Entry

A little sunshine. A lot of celebration.

Bring playful movement and joyful energy to your Haldi or Mehndi arrival with an entry designed around the mood of your celebration.

Plan My Haldi & Mehndi Entry

Possibilities to discuss

  • Couple and family entrances
  • Lively group choreography
  • Folk-inspired or contemporary performance concepts
  • Guest participation
  • Concepts suited to daytime settings

These are directions to explore together, not automatic inclusions.

How it works.

  1. Tell us your plans

    Share the occasion, people involved, and your ideas.

  2. Shape the concept

    We propose a direction and discuss what it involves.

  3. Confirm the details

    Scheduling, inclusions, and availability are agreed together.

Good to know.

Can guests take part?

Guest participation is a direction we can explore together, depending on your celebration.

Does an enquiry confirm a booking?

No. An enquiry starts a conversation. Availability and inclusions are confirmed during consultation.
