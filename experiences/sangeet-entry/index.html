Experiences / Sangeet Entry

An entrance that sets the celebration in motion.

Make your Sangeet arrival part of the performance. We design conceptual entries that bring together personality, music, and movement to introduce your evening.

Plan My Sangeet Entry

Possibilities to discuss

  • Bride, groom, or couple entries
  • Family and friends joining the entrance
  • Story-led or theme-led choreography
  • Professional dance ensemble participation
  • Music timing and entrance formations

These are directions to explore together, not automatic inclusions.

How it works.

  1. Tell us your plans

    Share the occasion, people involved, and your ideas.

  2. Shape the concept

    We propose a direction and discuss what it involves.

  3. Confirm the details

    Scheduling, inclusions, and availability are agreed together.

Good to know.

Can the whole family join the entrance?

Yes, family and friends can be part of an entry. We shape the choreography around who is taking part.

Does an enquiry confirm a booking?

No. An enquiry starts a conversation. Availability and inclusions are confirmed during consultation.
