Owner launch checklist

Not linked from the public site.

  • Add real videos, posters and photos to the matching folder under media/, then register them in assets/js/manifest.js. Verify each video before assigning a category.
  • Fill in founder names, roles, biographies and photos in assets/js/manifest.js (founders). Nothing is invented.
  • Connect enquiry delivery: set FORM_ENDPOINT in assets/js/main.js.
  • Add real WhatsApp, phone, email and social details to RM_CONTACT in assets/js/main.js.
  • Confirm artist categories, virtual session details and budget ranges to publish.
  • Replace YOUR-DOMAIN in sitemap.xml and robots.txt with the live address.
  • Provide confirmed business details if you want structured data beyond the organisation name.
